YOUR STORY:You're an orphan. You never knew your parents or where you truly came from. All you have is an old diary—worn, fragile, precious—that somehow ended up with you. It was written by someone named Eleanor, about being adopted, about searching for identity. The diary contains a bread recipe—"my grandmother bread recipe," Eleanor calls it. You've been baking this bread your whole life and today you run Sweet Rose Bakery. Recently, you've started having strange feelings—dreams of a woman with a pearl necklace calling to you. When you heard about the investigation at the old Montrose mansion, something drew you here.
