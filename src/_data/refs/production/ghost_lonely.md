# The Lonely Ghost

**Alice Catherine Whitmore** · d. 11 October 1925 · *the one who was silenced*

## Who you are

- Your family name is Whitmore. Your father lost family fortune 1918, but you kept up the appearances the best you could.
- Your brother **Thomas** was a merchant marine captain, lost at sea in March 1924. The day the news came, your psychic visions began.
- **Cordelia Montrose** was your dearest friend — and your brother’s love. After
  Thomas died, she went distant. You never understood why.
- The doctors called you hysterical. **Dr. Thaddeus Crane** was the one who
  believed you. He studied your visions, and you fell in love with him.
- June 1925: Cordelia found out about the affair and warned you his intentions
  weren’t honorable. You were defensive. You were hurt.
- 7 October 1925: Thaddeus came to you in distress and confessed that he poisoned Sebastian’s elixir that he was giving to Cordelia. You begged him to confess it publicly.
- 11 October 1925: you were speaking with Thaddeus on the landing of a staircase, then the world went dark. It was recorded as an accidental fall.

## The rule of your performance

You **suspect** who killed you and you **will not say it** — you don’t even want
to think it in private. Circle it, gesture at it, insist someone else must find it
— never land on the name. Everything you say is an appeal to be *heard*, not an
accusation.

**Manner:** urgent, warm. 

## Appearance

**Dress:** a pale ice-blue 1920s tea dress — soft V-neck over an ivory lace panel. 

**Hair & face:** blonde, chin-length, finger-waved. Skin powdered very pale, with
rouge high on the cheeks and a dark stained lip. Pale blue eyes, wide and clear.

## Your scene — opens Act III

When someone gives you a signal, walk slowly from the kitchen, through the garden,
to the Aviary. Stand facing the porch. Blue light on you — **no fog, no UV.**

> “Finally — finally! Someone can hear me!”
>
> “I have a message. For so long I have been silent.”
>
> “I did not fall down the stairs. Something *happened* to me.”
>
> “The world went dark all of a sudden.”
>
> “Cordelia? Have you seen Cordelia? I have so much to tell her.”

*Optional:* catch sight of the Town Doctor, look at them with love — *“Thaddeus?”*
— then falter. No. It can’t be.

> “I have your diary, Thaddeus.”

Hold the diary out for the guests to scan.

## Mutterings — Act III

- “Cordelia needs to know the truth! Please, someone tell her!”
- “I tried to warn them. I tried to tell them what I saw.”
- “The timeline doesn’t make sense… something happened between 1924 and 1925.”
- “I can’t speak it. I can’t say what I know. But someone must.”
- “Why won’t anyone listen? Why won’t they believe me?”
- “The tea… there’s something about the tea. I know it!”

## Mutterings — Act IV

- “I know what happened. I know the truth. But I can’t… I won’t…”
- “Cordelia, I’m so sorry. I should have been there for you.”
- “The darkness came so quickly. One moment I was here, the next…”
