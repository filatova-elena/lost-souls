# Run of Show

**Event: 7:00 – 9:00 PM**

Times from 7:20 onward are approximate and flow from the preceding beat. G.G.
paces the evening and uses the doorbell and the PA to call the house together.

---

# ARRIVAL & GREETING — 7:00

## 7:00 — Arrival

**Lead: The Housekeeper**

Guests arrive and are greeted at the door. The character cards are laid out on a
table.

> **Housekeeper:** "Welcome, we've been expecting you!"
>
> **Housekeeper:** "Please select your calling card." *(gestures to the table
> where the character cards are laid out)*

Each guest scans the QR code on their card. The Housekeeper makes sure they **tap
the button and see the confirmation animation** — a scan alone does not bind them
to the character.

Once confirmed, guests enter the house and read their card. The back carries their
background.

**Notes**
- Lay out only as many cards as there are guests, arranged so each required
  character gets taken.
- Required: The Baker, The Heiress, The Fiduciary, The Art Collector, The Town
  Doctor, The Explorer.
- The Baker must be taken. If needed, assign that character before the guest is
  welcomed into the foyer.

---

# ACT I — THE SETTING — 7:15

*The ghosts first appear when the doors open at ≈7:30.*

## 7:15 — G.G.'s Welcome

**Lead: G.G. (The Hostess)** — enters from the top of the staircase.

> **G.G.:** "Welcome, friends! Thank you for gathering here today! As you may have
> heard, the spirits who have been haunting the rooms of this house for a century
> have been restless lately. The Heiress *(may call out to the Heiress guest)*
> moved in and realized there is company."
>
> **G.G.:** "Something terrible happened in October of 1925: three young people
> died in the short span of 11 days, all connected to this mansion in one way or
> another. They have been haunting its halls ever since. Help us solve the
> century-old mystery and release these spirits!"
>
> **G.G.:** "Each of you are here for a reason, because you too are connected to
> this mansion whether you know it yet or not."

G.G. invites everyone to look at **the color of their card** and find the others
carrying the same color.

*[Optional: the Tea Server pours a glass of bubbly as the groups form.]*

---

## ≈7:20 — Groups Form and Introduce  *(10 min)*

Within each color group, guests introduce themselves **in character**: who they
are, and what drove them here tonight.

Their **objectives** are on the front of their cards.

**Note:** the four card colors are the four aspects — gossip, paranormal,
alchemical, adventure.

---

## ≈7:30 — The Doors Open / The Ghosts Appear

G.G. signals the Housekeeper to open the doors.

**Staging**
- The **Alchemist** stands in the **Library**, arms stretched toward the
  **Heartbroken Ghost**, who stands in the **dining room**.
- He calls out to her: *"Cordelia, forgive me!"*
- She glides slowly, eyes dreamy and detached, and does not notice him.
- The Heartbroken Ghost moves **upstairs**. The Alchemist follows.

> **G.G.:** "The spirits are especially restless tonight — they are eager for the
> truth."

---

## ≈7:33 — First Quest  *(≈12 min)*

G.G. sends the groups out to find their first clue — an **item**.

> **G.G.:** "Work in your groups to find your first clue. It is an object, and we
> need it. Scan the clues to make sure you have the right one before you bring it
> back — and look out for 🖐️."
>
> **G.G.:** "Once you have it, bring it to the piano room."

**Notes**
- The four objects: a Venetian glass bottle, an antique pocket watch, a wedding
  dress fragment belonging to the Heartbroken Ghost, and the Ginseng root the
  Alchemist wanders in search of.
- Guests may only touch a clue when the scan shows 🖐️.
- G.G. rings the doorbell to call everyone back to the piano room.

---

# ACT II — THE MYSTERY EMERGES — 7:45

## ≈7:45 — Piano Room: What We Learned  *(≈6 min)*

Everyone gathers in the piano room with the four items. G.G. asks each group in
turn to report; **one representative per group** shares what they learned from
their first clue.

> **G.G.:** "Thank you for gathering this important evidence."

---

## ≈7:51 — Free Investigation  *(≈12 min)*

> **G.G.:** "Wander the first floor of the mansion and look for more clues. And if
> you see a ghost — they may be trying to tell you something important. Listen to
> them."

---

# ACT III — THE INVESTIGATION — 8:03

## ≈8:03 — The Garden: The Lonely Ghost Appears

**Lead: G.G., then The Lonely Ghost**

G.G. calls everyone out to the garden.

> **G.G.** *(via PA)*: "I have an important announcement: the ghost of Alice
> Whitmore, the Lonely Ghost, has been spotted in the garden. She seems to have an
> important message for you."

**Staging**
- The Lonely Ghost walks slowly in from the kitchen, through the garden, to the
  Aviary, and stands facing the porch.
- Blue light on her pale skin and her pale ice-blue dress.
- **No fog machine, no black light** — both were cut.

> **The Lonely Ghost:** "Finally! Finally!! Someone can hear me! I have a message
> to share, for so long I've been silent! Cordelia, my friend. Have you seen
> Cordelia? I have so much to tell her."

*[Optional: The Lonely Ghost catches sight of the Town Doctor and looks at them
lovingly — "Thaddeus?!" — no, it can't be.]*

> **The Lonely Ghost** *(distracted)*: "I have your diary, Thaddeus."

The Lonely Ghost carries **Thaddeus's diary**, which bears a QR code. She offers
it out for the guests to scan.

> **G.G.:** "Scan the diary."

---

## ≈8:10 — The House Opens Up  *(≈10 min investigation)*

> **G.G.:** "There are more clues scattered through the garden and the carriage
> house — and upstairs as well."

**Staging:** one of the ghosts goes upstairs and stands in the window, pointing a
finger upward.

---

## ≈8:20 — Tea  *(10 min)*

**Lead: The Tea Server**, with G.G. calling them in. G.G. never names it as a
gossip break.

> **G.G.:** "Friends — come in, the tea is poured. Sit, rest your feet a moment."
>
> **G.G.:** "I have always found that a house gives up its secrets more easily
> over a warm cup. And I suspect every one of you has heard something tonight
> that you haven't yet said out loud."

The Tea Server works the room while pouring — warm, conspiratorial, thoroughly
nosy — spreading rumors and drawing guests from *different* groups into
conversation with each other.

> **Tea Server** *(leaning in with the pot)*: "Milk? Sugar? … And what have you
> heard?"
>
> **Tea Server:** "Now, you didn't hear this from me…"
>
> **Tea Server:** "That's not the version I was told. You two should talk —
> go on, tell them what you just told me."

**Note:** the Tea Server keeps circulating — nobody should talk only to their own
group.

---

# ACT IV — THE REVELATION — 8:30

## 8:30 — The Forensic Report / The Doors Unlock

**The doorbell rings.** The Forensic Scientist has returned with the analysis of
the elixir bottle collected earlier.

> **G.G.** *(via PA)*: "Everyone, please gather in the foyer immediately. The
> forensic scientist has returned with important findings."
>
> **Forensic Scientist:** "Thank you for gathering. I've completed my analysis of
> all four items you submitted. While each piece tells part of the story, the most
> significant finding comes from the Venetian glass bottle. The residue analysis
> reveals traces of *Digitalis purpurea* — foxglove. Here, read the forensic
> report for yourself."

Guests scan the QR code for the **Forensic Report — Elixir Bottle Analysis
(DM133)**.

*[If the Forensic Scientist is not cast, G.G. delivers the findings herself.]*

**At the same time:** the Housekeeper unlocks the kitchen and Neil's bedroom,
which have been locked all evening. G.G. announces it.

> **G.G.:** "Two doors in this house have been locked all evening. They are open
> now — the kitchen, and the bedroom at the top of the stairs."

---

## ≈8:32 — The Last Clues and the Treasure  *(≈10 min)*

Guests move into the newly opened rooms for the final clues, then out to the
garden for the treasure.

**Notes**
- The treasure is buried **next to the rose planter**. The map is hidden in the
  purple and gold vase, and the lock code is **DRAKE** — Colonel Charles Drake,
  the name attached to the aviary.
- The chest holds chocolate coins.

---

# ACT V — CONCLUSIONS — 8:42

## ≈8:42 — Living Room: The Questions

G.G. calls everyone into the living room and puts one question to each group in
turn.

> **G.G.:** "Before we reveal the complete truth, let us test your
> investigation."

**Gossip — Secret Baby**
> *"Whose child was given away, and what does that mean for this estate?"*
>
> Eleanor Sullivan was the secret daughter of Cordelia Montrose and Thomas
> Whitmore. The Montrose family forced Cordelia to give the baby away to the
> Sullivans. The adoption was never finalized — Eleanor is the true heir to the
> Montrose estate.

**Alchemical — Sebastian's Elixir**
> *"What was truly in the Alchemist's formula?"*
>
> The Elixir of Eternal Love was a harmless romantic potion. His brother, Dr.
> Thaddeus Crane, secretly added concentrated foxglove, turning it into a
> slow-acting poison. Sebastian died testing his own creation, never knowing his
> brother had corrupted it. Cordelia died of six weeks of accumulated poisoning.

**Paranormal — Alice Whitmore**
> *"Who silenced the Lonely Ghost, and why?"*
>
> Alice was a genuine psychic who fell in love with her doctor, Thaddeus Crane.
> When Cordelia discovered the affair and threatened to expose him, he poisoned
> Sebastian's elixir to silence her. Alice learned the truth and demanded he
> confess. He murdered her with a silver candle holder on the staircase and
> staged it as a fall.

**Adventure — Dirty Dealings**
> *"What was hidden on these grounds, and who hid it?"*
>
> The Romano treasure is real, hidden across the estate by Frankie Romano, who
> used Sebastian's apothecary as a front for smuggling during Prohibition. He
> left a coded trail — One, Two, Three — from the Methuselah Palm to the
> paintings to the purple vase to the aviary.

---

## ≈8:52 — Conclusions / The Heir

> **G.G.:** "Thank you, investigators. You have uncovered the truth that has been
> hidden for a century. Dr. Thaddeus Crane's terrible secret has finally been
> revealed."

G.G. names **The Baker** as the heir to the Montrose estate — Eleanor's
descendant, carrying her rose bread recipe and her bloodline.

---

## ≈8:55 — The Staircase: The Release

G.G. moves to the staircase. The ghosts have already gathered at the top.

She asks everyone to repeat after her. G.G. speaks each line; the room repeats it
back before she moves to the next.

> The glass remembers
> Confessions bound
> Walk to the sea
> In truth, be free

The ghosts are released. They walk out the front door.

**9:00 — Evening concludes.**
