# Send the reminder from Eventbrite

1. Open `reminder_email.html` in Chrome. You should see the finished email, with the
   QR code. (No QR code? The website isn't live yet. Stop and ask.)
2. Press **Cmd+A**, then **Cmd+C**.
3. Eventbrite → your event → **Manage attendees** → **Emails to attendees** → new email.
   - **To:** all attendees
   - **Reply-to:** an address you read
   - **Subject:** `One small ritual before Lost Souls`
   - **Message:** click in the box and press **Cmd+V**. The text, button and QR code come across ready.
4. **Send a test to yourself.** Open it on your phone, tap the button, and check the QR code shows.
5. **Schedule** it for about a week before the event.

Late buyers don't get it, so send it again to them later.
