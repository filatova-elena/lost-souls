"Welcome, we've been expecting you!"

"Please select your calling card." (points to the table with character cards)

"Scan the QR code to read about the past you've forgotten, and then click the button at the bottom."

"Once you've done that, please sign in by scanning the QR code in the book."

Notes

The Lonely Ghost

Alice Whitmore

Scene — opens Act III

Walk slowly from the kitchen, through the garden, to the Aviary. Stand facing the porch.

"Finally, finally, someone can hear me!"

"I have a message for you."

"I did not fall down the stairs — something happened to me."

"The world went dark all of a sudden."

"Thaddeus? Have you seen him?"

"I have his diary."

"Time for our true feelings to be known."

Then offer the diary out to the guests to scan.

Instructions

  • Silently offer people vision cards to scan — by act!
  • Optionally, improvise short mutterings.

Mutterings

Act III

  • "Cordelia needs to know the truth! Please, someone tell her!"
  • "I tried to warn them. I tried to tell them what I saw."
  • "The timeline doesn't make sense… something happened between 1924 and 1925."
  • "I can't speak it. I can't say what I know. But someone must."
  • "Something is wrong. I can feel it in my bones."
  • "Why won't anyone listen? Why won't they believe me?"
  • "The tea… there's something about the tea. I know it!"

The Heartbroken Ghost

Cordelia Montrose

Scene — Act I

When G.G. says "let the investigation begin" and the doors open:

  • Cordelia stands in the middle of the dining room, slowly walking toward the pantry/kitchen.
  • Sebastian is in the Library, looking at her. He says: "Cordelia, forgive me!"
  • Cordelia slowly looks at him, and walks away.

Instructions

  • Silently offer people vision cards to scan — by act!
  • Optionally, improvise short mutterings.

Mutterings

Act I

  • "I think about Thomas. My first love. Lost at sea years ago."
  • "I remember Elias, he used to write me poems…"
  • "Alice. Dear, brilliant Alice — my dear friend! Are you coming for tea tonight?!"
  • "The roses in the garden… they remind me of happier times."

Act II

  • "My head aches… why does everything feel so heavy?"
  • "Sebastian, where are you? I need you here with me."
  • "Something isn't right. I can't think clearly anymore."
  • "The elixir tasted sweet this morning… but why do I feel so weak?"

Act III

  • "Alice… where did you go? I called for you but you never came."
  • "Sebastian is gone too. Everyone I love leaves me. Why?"
  • "I'm so tired. So very tired. My heart feels wrong."
  • "There are so many unanswered questions… so many secrets."

Act IV

  • "THE GLASS REMEMBERS!" — around the time the Forensic Scientist arrives.

The Alchemist

Scene — Act I

  • Cordelia stands in the middle of the dining room, slowly walking toward the pantry/kitchen.
  • Sebastian is in the Library, looking at her. He says: "Cordelia, forgive me!"
  • Cordelia slowly looks at him, and walks away.

Instructions

  • Silently offer people vision cards to scan — by act!
  • Optionally, improvise short mutterings.

Mutterings

Act I

  • "Love can be perfected through chemistry… I know it can."
  • "The stars align, the formula is flawless. This will be my masterpiece."
  • "Cordelia understands my vision. She sees the poetry in science."
  • "Damiana, Valerian, Rose Otto… each ingredient chosen with care."

Act II

  • "Cordelia is getting sick… but my formula is flawless. It must be stress from the wedding."
  • "Everyone keeps worrying, but they don't understand chemistry like I do. My work is perfect."
  • "My brother says it's nerves. Women are delicate, after all. It's probably nothing serious."
  • "I've spent years perfecting this. Years! I know what I'm doing. They just don't understand."

Act III

  • "I drank it myself. I had to know… I had to be certain."
  • "Something is terribly wrong. My heart… it won't stop racing."
  • "I failed her. I failed my great work. How could I be so wrong?"
  • "My brother tried to help… but nothing worked. Nothing could save me."
